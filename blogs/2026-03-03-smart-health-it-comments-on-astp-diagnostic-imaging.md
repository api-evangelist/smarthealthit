By JOSHUA MANDEL, MD, DAN GOTTLIEB, MPA, and KENNETH D. MANDL, MD, MPH We strongly support ASTP/ONC’s objective of expanding standards-based, API-driven interoperability to include diagnostic imaging. The market today is highly fragmented, relying on physical media and proprietary viewer portals that are not accessible via standards-based APIs.
